Gastritis refers to a range of conditions that cause inflammation of the stomach lining. Antral gastritis specifically is an inflammation of the antrum, the lower part of the stomach that connects to the small intestine.

This inflammation can be caused by various factors, including:

Infections (such as the bacterium Helicobacter pylori)

An autoimmune disease

Heavy or excessive alcohol consumption

Long-term use of nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs)

Stress

The combination of one or any of these factors could result in antral gastritis.
