How can you identify a penile fracture visually?

A penile fracture can be identified by a sudden popping sound, pain, swelling, and sometimes a bent appearance of the penis.

A penile fracture occurs when there is a tear in the tunica albuginea, the tough covering of the penile tissue. This usually happens during vigorous sexual activity or trauma.

Signs to Look For:

Popping Sound: Often, a person hears or feels a sudden pop or crack.

Pain: There is immediate and severe pain in the penis.

Swelling and Bruising: The penis may swell and show bruising, which can make it look like an eggplant, a condition known as the "eggplant sign."

Bent Appearance: The penis might appear bent or deformed.

Urethral Bleeding: Sometimes, there might be blood at the tip of the penis if the urethra is also injured.
