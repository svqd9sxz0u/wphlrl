Restless leg syndrome (RLS) can cause uncomfortable feelings in your legs, especially at night. If these feelings are very strong, keep you from sleeping, or make it hard to do daily activities, it's a good idea to see a doctor.

When to See a Doctor:

Severe Symptoms: If the feelings in your legs are very strong or painful.

Sleep Problems: If you can't sleep well because of the symptoms.

[See Doctor for RLS](https://nootropic-atlas.escortskhi.com/blog/9171157556.html)

Daily Life Impact: If you find it hard to do things you enjoy or need to do.

No Improvement: If self-care tips like stretching or cutting back on caffeine don't help.

Doctors can help by finding out if something else is causing your symptoms and suggesting treatments to help you feel better.
