Tense neck muscles, whiplash, and a pinched nerve are among the most common causes of neck pain. Muscle tension often stems from poor posture or stress, while whiplash typically follows sudden impact injuries. A pinched nerve occurs when surrounding tissue compresses a nerve, causing pain, tingling, or weakness.

Because these conditions share overlapping symptoms but require different treatments, identifying the underlying cause matters. [Neck Pain on One](https://musclemend.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/8346007173.html)

If there is pain only on one side of the neck, it is primarily due to abnormalities that occur in the muscles or nerves around the neck. Specifically, the following causes can be considered.

[Ignore These Urgent](https://musclemend.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/0707086653.html)

Tense Neck Muscles

When an unnatural posture of the neck is maintained for a long time during sleep, using your smartphone, muscles can become tense on one side. Tense muscles can lead to pain and stiffness on only one side of the neck when you wake up or having been doing some activity for an extended period of time.

Whiplash

Whiplash injuries can occur when the head is jerked back and forth due to a rear-end collision or similar accidents, causing damage to the muscles around the cervical spine. If the impact occurs more on one side, it is possible that pain may be more pronounced on that side.

Pinched Nerve

The nerves in the neck can be compressed by the cervical vertebrae or intervertebral discs, causing pain on one side of the neck. The pain often spreads not only to the neck but also to the shoulders, back, and arms.
