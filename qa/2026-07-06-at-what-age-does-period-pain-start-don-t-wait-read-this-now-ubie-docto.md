Explanation

Menstrual pain, also known as dysmenorrhea, is a common issue among young women and often begins with the onset of menstruation. Many women start experience period pain during their teenage years.

Age of Onset: Typically, girls start having menstrual cycles around the ages of 11 to 14, and this is often when period pain begins.

Prevalence: A significant number of young women experience some level of menstrual pain, which can vary in intensity.

[What Age](https://matrix-skin.xianfengchem.com/blog/8054663273.html)

Management: Approaches to managing menstrual pain can include lifestyle changes, over-the-counter pain relief (such as ibuprofen, naproxen, acetaminophen, or paracetamol), and other methods as suggested by healthcare providers.

Understanding that period pain is a common part of growing up can help young women and their families prepare and manage these symptoms effectively.
