What does it mean if I'm throwing up blood while pregnant?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Vomiting blood during pregnancy (hematemesis) is a serious symptom that requires prompt medical evaluation. Common causes include severe morning sickness (hyperemesis gravidarum), acute pancreatitis, esophageal tears from repeated vomiting, gastritis, or peptic ulcers. Identifying the underlying cause early helps protect both maternal and fetal health.

Because the causes vary widely in severity, understanding your specific symptoms is critical before deciding on next steps.
