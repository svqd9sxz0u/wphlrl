# Wait, Did Amy Poehler Just Tease a 'Parks and Rec' Revival?

Fans of NBC sitcom Parks & Recreation could find themselves returning to the oddball town of Pawnee, Indiana at some point in the future. Speaking at the recent cast reunion at PaleyFest, series star Amy Poehler said that if and when show-runner Michael Schur becomes available, she’d love to revisit those characters.

Parks & Rec started out as a workplace sitcom focusing on a small-town government worker, and wove plenty of political satire into its seven seasons, including an election storyline which would turn out to be eerily prescient in 2016. “The show started very hopeful, in a post-Obama time,” Poehler said. “It would be interesting to start it now.”

Comedian Patton Oswalt, who had a recurring guest spot on the show, suggested that Parks & Rec movie might be the perfect way to bring the team back together. However, Michael Schur (who’s currently busy working on cosmic comedy The Good Place) said: “I think everyone on this stage would want to feel there’s a story to be told. The show was about the power of public service and doing good with a team… I don’t think we left anything on the table.”

He has a point; the show’s final season ended with protagonist Leslie Knope (Poehler) leaving Pawnee to pursue her dream of a political career in Washington, D.C. However, Schur did add: “I would never say never,” while Poehler confirmed that she would only do a revival if Schur were involved.

“I have no idea where I’d want it to go, we would do what we usually do: blow out the formula, do nothing that works, put us in space!” She joked. “This is why I’m not the one with the ideas… We need our captain, for sure.”
