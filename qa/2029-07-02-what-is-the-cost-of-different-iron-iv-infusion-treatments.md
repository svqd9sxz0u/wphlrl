Why Get IV Infusion Treatments?

Many people are deficient in iron and require iron supplementation in order to feel their best. Low iron levels can be associated with feelings of fatigue, shortness of breath, dizziness, and lightheadedness. If your doctor finds that you have low iron levels, then getting iron replacement via IV supplementation may be a helpful solution for you.

How to Prepare for an Iron Infusion

Before getting an iron infusion, you’ll need to have some bloodwork done to confirm that your iron levels are low enough to require an infusion. Having recent bloodwork done can also help your provider know the correct iron infusion dosage to order. Make sure you’ve done plenty of research regarding where you will get your infusion and determine a rough estimate of what it will cost. It is worth seeing if your health insurance can cover any portion of your iron IV infusion treatments.

On the day of your iron infusion, you can eat breakfast and take your typical medications (unless your doctor has stated otherwise). Your infusion could take 3 to 4 hours, so make sure to bring some water to drink, a book to read, your phone charger, and any other essential items you may need. Wear comfortable clothing and be prepared to have an IV placed in your arm.
