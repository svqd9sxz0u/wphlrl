Pus in the urethra is often a sign that there is something wrong, like an infection or a foreign object. According to the provided references, pus can be caused by issues such as a broken piece of a catheter left in the urethra, which might lead to infection or irritation. Urethral discharge can also be related to other conditions, like constipation or hemorrhoids, which may affect the urethra indirectly.

Symptoms of Pus in the Urethra:

Discomfort or Pain: You might feel pain when you urinate at the urethra opening or in the lower abdominal area.

Unusual Discharge: There could be a yellow or greenish fluid coming from the urethra, which is the pus.
