A cortisol cocktail is a drink often consumed to help balance cortisol, the body's main stress hormone. The best time to drink one is typically in the morning, when cortisol levels naturally peak, or in the afternoon during an energy dip. However, timing may vary based on your caffeine sensitivity, sleep patterns, and adrenal health. For personalized guidance, consult a healthcare provider.

Explanation

Cortisol is a hormone that helps your body respond to stress, and its levels naturally vary throughout the day. According to the available references, caffeine can stimulate cortisol secretion, which might be why some people refer to a 'cortisol cocktail.' However, there is no specific mention of an ideal time to consume such a cocktail in these studies.

Caffeine and Cortisol: One study discusses how caffeine can increase cortisol levels, but it doesn't specify the best time of day to consume it.

Meal Timing and Metabolism: Another study mentions meal timing and its effects on blood sugar but does not relate it directly to cortisol levels.

Cortisol Levels in Menopause: A third study examines cortisol during menopause but does not address the timing of cortisol cocktails.
