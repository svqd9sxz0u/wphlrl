Emergency Medicine

Your poop might smell like eggs because bacteria in your gut produce sulfur-rich gases during digestion.

Important Next Steps

Not seeing your question? No worries.

Explanation

Inside your gut, many tiny bacteria help you break down food. When the bacteria break down foods rich in sulfur like eggs or some vegetables, they produce gases that contain sulfur, like hydrogen sulfide, which smells similar to rotten eggs. This egg-like odor in your poop is often a normal result of how your body digests food. However, if the egg-like smell lasts a long time or comes with other symptoms like stomach pain or diarrhea, it could be a sign of a gut issue that you might want to discuss with a healthcare provider.
