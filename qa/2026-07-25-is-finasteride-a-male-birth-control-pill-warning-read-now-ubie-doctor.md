Is finasteride basically a male birth control pill?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Finasteride is not a male birth control pill and should not be used for contraception. It does not significantly affect sperm production, sperm count, or sperm function. Finasteride is FDA-approved to treat male pattern hair loss and benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) by blocking the conversion of testosterone to DHT—it does not act as a contraceptive.
