Explanation

The appearance of having no ankles, sometimes referred to as 'cankles,' can be due to a few different reasons. According to the medical literature, one possible cause is a decrease in the muscle volume around the foot and ankle. This means that the muscles in these areas may be smaller or less developed, making the ankles appear less defined.

Another reason could be related to the alignment of the lower limbs. If the alignment is affected, such as in cases of primary valgus ankle, where the ankle tilts inward, it can change the way the ankle looks.
