Biotin injections have been studied for improved nail and hair growth for some time. While biotin (aka vitamin B-7) can help, it’s most beneficial for those who have medical issues like alopecia causing a biotin deficiency or other related underlying cause of hair loss/brittle nails.

In other words, if your hair loss is not caused by an underlying medical condition, a biotin injection is unlikely to result in significant hair regrowth.

As for risks, the body flushes out unneeded biotin, so it’s unlikely that a healthy person would overdose on it. However, it is possible to have negative side effects from Biotin, especially if you suffer from health issues that could be caused by low vitamin C and vitamin B-6 levels. People with or at risk of diabetes should be especially cautious as too-high biotin levels can cause high blood sugar and a decline in insulin production.
