Does acupuncture work for curing overactive bladder (OAB)?

Reviewed by Suo Lee, MD

Internal Medicine

Acupuncture may help reduce the symptoms of overactive bladder but it is not considered a cure.

Important Next Steps

Not seeing your question? No worries.

Explanation

Acupuncture is an ancient technique where very thin needles are placed in different parts of the body. Some studies show that acupuncture can help lessen overactive bladder symptoms like frequent urges to urinate and leakage. The idea is that the needles help relax nerves and muscles in the bladder area, which can reduce urgency and discomfort. However, while many adults report some benefit from acupuncture, the research does not show that it completely cures overactive bladder. Instead, it is seen as one way to manage the symptoms when used along with other treatments. Also, not everyone experiences the same improvement because each person’s body reacts differently. If you are thinking about trying acupuncture, it is important to talk with your doctor or a trained acupuncturist first. They can help you understand what to expect and decide if it might be a good addition to your overall treatment plan for overactive bladder.
