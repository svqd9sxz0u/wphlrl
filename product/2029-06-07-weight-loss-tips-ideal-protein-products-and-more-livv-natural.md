NAD+

NAD+ is a coenzyme found in every living cell. It stands for nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. It supports cellular metabolism by converting food into fuel for cells.

This molecule helps you stay healthy and fit. It plays an integral role in maintaining the integrity of your DNA. It also protects your cells from aging and disease.

How exactly does NAD+ work? It functions similarly to a shuttle bus, transporting electrons to molecules within cells. Along the way, it shares reactions and processes for the cells to follow.

NAD+ regulates metabolic reactions that determine how the cells generate energy. It also controls the circadian rhythm and maintains your body’s sleep and wake cycle.

Without NAD+, cells struggle to generate energy, and your body would be on the fast track to death. As you age, NAD+ levels naturally decline, but taking a supplement can keep them elevated.

NAD+ could boost your body’s ability to burn calories and help you lose weight quicker. It can engage cells to burn off more food for fuel and prevent excess fat storage.

Epithalon

Epithalon is an anti-aging peptide. It works by lengthening telomeres, DNA sequences at the end of chromosomes.

As cells age, the telomeres get shorter. It affects cell communication and eventually leads to the death of cells.

Cells that can’t communicate won’t be able to generate sufficient energy for fuel. In other words, they won’t burn as many calories. This effect leads to weight gain over time.

Epithalon regulates the enzyme telomerase, which has a protective effect on the telomeres. Keeping the cells in peak condition helps them burn more food for fuel. It also improves communication.

Hormones

Hormones influence your appetite, metabolism, and fat distribution. To maintain a healthy weight, your hormones need to remain in their delicate balance.

Women’s hormones naturally change with reproductive age and menopause. An imbalance can cause symptoms like hot flashes, fatigue, acne, and weight gain.

Estrogen is produced in the ovaries. It balances the thyroid hormones for female reproductive organs. It promotes fat storage, which is meant to help with reproduction.

Too much or too little estrogen stimulates excessive weight gain. Women can try hormonal therapy or natural methods to rebalance their hormones.

A lack of testosterone is also a common cause of weight problems for both women and men. Low testosterone levels cause hair loss, weight gain, and reduced muscle mass.

Testosterone therapy may help men balance their hormones. It aids in the production of more muscle than fat.

Fill up on veg

Vegetables are full of fiber, antioxidants, and vitamins. They add volume to your plate and make you feel satisfied.

Ideally, you should consume about 2.5 cups daily. Vegetables also have a high water content that can make you feel full quicker.

Vegetables are also low in calories. You can eat more of them without pounding on the calories. It’s extremely beneficial to volume eaters who need large servings for satiety.

Be wary of starchy vegetables like potatoes. They’re high in carbohydrates and calories. High-carb veg can form part of a healthy diet as long as you watch the portions.

The method you cook vegetables with can affect their nutritional value and calories. Avoid frying them in too much oil or butter. A short steam is the best way to preserve the vitamins and keep them light.

Certain vegetables can also make ideal protein products. High-protein veg, like chickpeas and beans, deliver affordable protein sources. They’re an excellent choice for vegetarians, people on a budget, or those on a weight loss journey.

Many people struggle to eat more vegetables when they’re on a weight loss journey. Here are some ways to enjoy veggies when satisfaction is a big part of healthy eating:

Enjoy a salad with finely chopped veg in a balsamic vinaigrette or apple cider dressing.

Steam and blend vegetables and infuse them into sauces for pasta or gravies.

Roast vegetables in extra virgin olive oil and garlic for a perfect side.

Make a veggie dip like hummus or baba ganoush. Use vegetable slices as chips.

Carbs are not the enemy

Diet culture has made most people afraid of carbohydrates. When it’s time to get your summer body ready, bagels become the devil. It doesn’t have to be that way.

Carbohydrates are a macronutrient, similar to proteins and fats. They’re your body’s preferred fuel source. Your brain and red blood cells almost exclusively use carbohydrates for energy.

Bagels, pizza, and white rice can be part of a healthy, balanced diet. When you’re trying to lose weight, you can enjoy these foods occasionally. The key is to watch your portion size.

When it comes to daily carbohydrate intake, choose complex carbs. Good options include quinoa, oats, and whole wheat breads. They have a higher fiber content that eases digestion and prevents constipation. They also increase satiety and keep blood sugar levels stable.

Always pair carbs with ideal protein products like chicken breasts. It slows down digestion and keeps you full for longer. It also prevents a blood sugar spike.

Aim for 100–150 grams of carbohydrates a day to lose weight. Ensure you consider non-obvious carbs, like vegetables and fruits.

Enjoy healthy fats

Fat doesn’t make you fat. As controversial as that sounds, it’s true.

According to the American Dietary Association, dietary fats keep you healthy. They promote cell growth and aid hormone production. Fats also help keep your body warm and protect some of your organs.

However, not all fats are good. Trans fats found commonly in processed foods are bad for your health. They raise cholesterol, clog your arteries, and trigger inflammation. These fats also cause weight gain. They increase your chances of developing a metabolic condition.

Monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fats hold health benefits. They’re commonly found in avocados, olive oil, and sesame. Eating a small portion of these fats daily keeps you healthy. It also aids weight loss by keeping you satisfied and full.

While fats are good for you, you should consume them in small amounts. They’re calorie-dense foods that can cause weight gain if you eat too much. Aim for 33–58 grams of healthy fats daily.

Exercise consistently

One of the top tips for maximizing fat loss is regular exercise. Regular physical activity uses up more calories that your body would otherwise store.

Eating low-calorie meals and exercising puts you in a caloric deficit. In other words, you burn more calories than you consume.

Exercise is the best way to maintain a healthy weight. A diet may make you shed a few pounds, but exercise will help you keep it off.

Consistent exercise won’t just help you lose weight. It also plays an important role in keeping your heart healthy. It can protect you from getting metabolic conditions like diabetes.

Aim for at least 300 minutes of moderately intense activity a week to lose weight. Avoid setting unrealistic exercise goals. Beginners should try easy workouts, like brisk walking, for 30 minutes a day.

High-intensity interval training (HIIT) is an excellent workout regime. It scorches calories in a shorter amount of time. It also keeps your body in fat-burning mode for up to 24 hours after the workout.

Cold exposure is another excellent way to lose weight. It stimulates the production of brown fat, which easily converts fat into energy.

Enjoy a dip in an ice bath and go for a 30 minute swim. It won’t just help you lose weight, it’ll also improve your mood.

Strength training can help you build muscle and lose fat simultaneously. Aim for 45 minutes to an hour of weight lifting at least three days a week.

Regular exercise is important, but so is rest. Ensure you have at least two rest days between your workout splits. It allows your body to relax and repair. It also prevents inflammation and hormonal imbalances.
