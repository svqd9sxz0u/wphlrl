What is TRT?

TRT is a non-invasive medical treatment that involves replacing the testosterone hormone in the body. Testosterone plays an important role in maintaining muscle mass and strength, regulating bone density, supporting sexual function and libido, enhancing cognitive function, regulating emotions, and providing energy.But as men age, testosterone levels can decline, causing symptoms such as fatigue, lack of sexual function, low libido, changes in mood, and more. Over time, the symptoms can begin to affect your quality of life. Low T can also be caused by chronic illnesses, stress, unhealthy lifestyle habits, and certain medications.TRT treatments can replenish your testosterone levels, helping you to feel healthy, strong, and like yourself again.
