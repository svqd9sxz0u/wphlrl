# Amazon Will Soon Let You Try Clothes Before You Buy

[Soon Let](https://glp-insights.xianfengchem.com/blog/4818307812.html)

While Amazon was busy buying Whole Foods earlier this week, you might have another update from the online retailer: the rollout of its new try-on fashion service, Prime Wardrobe. Here’s how it works.

[Before You Buy](https://tendonforge.dns0663.com/)

Included in your Prime membership, Prime Wardrobe will let you order a box with three or more qualifying style items with free shipping, as usual. You can try everything on for a week, pack up whatever you're not digging in the box with a prepaid shipping label. After the return, you’ll only pay for what you keep.

This type of business model isn’t new (see Trunk Club, Stitch Fix, Five Four Club), but in true Amazon fashion, this service includes additional perks that only the retail giant can offer. For example, more than one million items that qualify for Prime Wardrobe, including brands such as Levi's, Adidas, Calvin Klein, and Timex.
