CHRISTIAN MCCAFFREY MAY may not have suited up for the Super Bowl, but he showed up everywhere else that weekend. The 49ers running back hit the social circuit hard, bouncing between the Abercrombie party to Thom Browne’s Fall 2026 show. Anchoring his looks was a sharp vintage 1940s Movado watch sourced through eBay.

It’s a smart style move, especially considering most of McCaffrey’s peers show up to big events with even bigger watches. This midcentury Movado is low-key but loaded with thoughtful details. The dial features day and month windows, a date track along the outer rim, and a bright orange pointer hand that sweeps the perimeter to mark the date—a classic 1940s touch. The slim 18k yellow gold case paired with a dark brown leather strap feels just as right with a Thom Browne suit as it does with jeans and a jacket.
