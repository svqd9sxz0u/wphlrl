Yes, mobile IV infusions do come with potential side effects, but for most people, they are mild and temporary. The most common complications we see and treat include mild bruising at the injection site, slight swelling, and temporary dizziness. Your nurse will have all the training and equipment necessary to intervene immediately with solutions that keep you safe and comfortable.
