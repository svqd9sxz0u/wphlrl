Action movie legend Sylvester Stallone is back in the gym, recovering from the shoot for Rambo 5: Last Blood. In an Instagram post, the 72-year-old actor said that last year’s grueling filming schedule in Bulgaria did “a lot of damage” which he’s now working to repair—and he’s doing it with a positive attitude.

“It’s been a long time since I’ve been in the gym because I’ve been out there doing Rambo, which caused a lot of damage. So now I’ve got to fix things,” he said.

“Coming back here, let’s face it, it’s not easy,” he continued. “Who wouldn’t rather be in bed just relaxing a little bit? But listen. Life is like a movie; you got to star in your own feature. And if you want to play the lead, you got to get out there and go after it.”
