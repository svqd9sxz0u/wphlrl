Explanation

Testosterone is a hormone that helps our brain work well. It supports the release and balance of chemicals that help us think clearly and feel calm. When testosterone levels are low, the brain may not send signals as well. This can make it harder for someone to pay attention in school or work.

[Why Focus Anxiety](https://metabolic-horizon.bbsxyxy.com/)

Research in animals shows that lower testosterone changes how the brain controls stress and fear, leading to more anxious behaviors. Studies in both men and women suggest that low testosterone is linked with higher stress levels and less concentration.

In simple words, without enough testosterone, the natural "helper" role it plays in our brain can weaken, making it more difficult to concentrate and increasing feelings of worry or anxiety.
