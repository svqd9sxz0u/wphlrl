Partner Wellness: Helping Your Partner Manage Health Concerns During Your Pregnancy

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Testicular pain in a male partner during pregnancy can affect the whole family's wellness. Common causes include muscle strain, infections, or stress-related tension, and relief often comes from rest, supportive underwear, cold packs, and stress reduction. However, sudden severe pain, swelling, fever, or nausea are red flags that require urgent medical care.

Key things to know:

Infections may need antibiotics and prompt evaluation

Intimacy patterns and emotional stress during pregnancy can influence symptoms

Preventive health steps benefit both partners

Persistent or worsening symptoms warrant a clinician visit

Explanation

Pregnancy is often seen as a time when the focus shifts entirely to the expectant mother. But your partner's health matters just as much. When one partner is pregnant, both people experience physical, emotional, and lifestyle changes. Supporting each other through this time strengthens your relationship and helps create a healthier environment for your growing baby.

One concern that sometimes comes up—but isn't often talked about—is male reproductive discomfort, including testicular pain. While it may feel awkward to bring up, it's important not to ignore it. Understanding how to relieve testicular pain, when it's normal, and when it's serious can help both of you feel more confident and supported during pregnancy.

Why Partner Health Matters During Pregnancy

Your partner's well-being affects:

Emotional stability in the household

Stress levels for both of you

Sexual health and intimacy

The ability to provide physical and practical support

When health issues—big or small—are addressed early, it prevents unnecessary stress during a time that already brings change.

Common Health Concerns for Male Partners During Pregnancy

Partners may experience:

Increased stress or anxiety

Sleep disturbances

Weight gain or appetite changes

Reduced physical activity

Hormonal shifts (yes, this can happen in men too)

Reproductive discomfort, including testicular pain

Some men experience physical symptoms during their partner's pregnancy, sometimes referred to as "couvade syndrome." These symptoms are usually mild and temporary, but pain should always be evaluated carefully.

Understanding Testicular Pain

Testicular pain can range from mild discomfort to severe, sudden pain. It may affect one or both testicles.

Common Causes Include:

Minor injury or strain

Muscle tension from stress

Epididymitis (inflammation, often due to infection)

Varicocele (enlarged veins in the scrotum)

Inguinal hernia

Kidney stones

Testicular torsion (a medical emergency)

Most causes are treatable. The key is knowing when pain is mild and manageable—and when it requires urgent medical attention.

How to Relieve Testicular Pain (When It's Mild)

If pain is mild, gradual, and not accompanied by severe symptoms, these strategies may help:

✅ Rest and Limit Activity

Avoid heavy lifting.

Reduce strenuous exercise.

Take breaks from prolonged standing.

✅ Use Supportive Underwear

Wear snug (not tight) supportive underwear.

Athletic supporters can reduce strain.

✅ Apply Cold Packs

Use a cold pack wrapped in a towel.

Apply for 10–20 minutes at a time.

Do not apply ice directly to skin.

✅ Over-the-Counter Pain Relief

Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs), such as ibuprofen, may help reduce inflammation and pain.

Always follow dosing instructions.

If there are medical conditions or medication interactions, consult a doctor first.

✅ Elevate the Scrotum While Lying Down

Place a folded towel underneath for support.

When Testicular Pain Is an Emergency

Certain symptoms require immediate medical attention:

Sudden, severe testicular pain

Swelling or redness

Nausea or vomiting with pain

Fever

Pain following trauma

One testicle sitting higher than the other

Pain that doesn't improve within an hour

These may indicate testicular torsion, which happens when the spermatic cord twists and cuts off blood supply. This is a medical emergency and can cause permanent damage if not treated quickly.

Do not wait. Seek urgent care or go to the emergency room immediately.

Infection-Related Testicular Pain

One common cause of ongoing discomfort is epididymitis, an inflammation often caused by bacterial infection. Symptoms may include:

Gradual onset of pain

Swelling

Burning during urination

Discharge

This condition requires antibiotics prescribed by a doctor. Delaying care can lead to complications, including fertility issues.

If infection is suspected, both partners may need evaluation depending on the cause.

The Bottom Line

Pregnancy is a shared journey. While much attention focuses on the mother, partner wellness is just as important. Issues like testicular pain may feel uncomfortable to discuss, but addressing them early protects long-term health and reduces stress for both of you.
