What Is Chronic Stress?

Chronic stress occurs when your fight-or-flight system stays activated for weeks or months. Common signs include:

Persistent fatigue or insomnia

Muscle tension and headaches

Mood swings, anxiety, or low motivation

Digestive upset (bloating, indigestion)

Weakened immunity

Over time, elevated cortisol (the stress hormone) can impair metabolism, sleep quality, and even cardiovascular health. Recognizing these patterns early is key to preventing more serious complications.
