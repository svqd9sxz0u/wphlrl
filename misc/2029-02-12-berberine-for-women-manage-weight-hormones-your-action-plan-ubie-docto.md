Safety, Side Effects & Interactions

Common Side Effects

Mild gastrointestinal upset (cramping, gas, diarrhea)

Headache or dizziness (usually transient)

How to minimize:

Take berberine with meals.

Start with a lower dose and increase gradually.

Potential Interactions

Medications for diabetes: Berberine can amplify blood-sugar–lowering effects. Monitor closely to avoid hypoglycemia.

Blood pressure drugs: May cause a stronger drop in blood pressure.

Anticoagulants (blood thinners): Berberine can increase bleeding risk if combined with warfarin or similar medications.

Always review supplements and prescription meds with your healthcare provider before starting berberine.

When to Seek Professional Advice

Blood sugar consistently below 70 mg/dL or recurring hypoglycemia symptoms (sweating, shakiness).

Severe or persistent gastrointestinal issues.

Signs of allergic reaction (rash, swelling, difficulty breathing).

If you're pregnant, breastfeeding, or have a serious chronic illness.
