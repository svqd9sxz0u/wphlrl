As the NBA approaches week eight of being suspended due to the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic, LeBron James has made no secret about the fact that he's strongly against the idea of canceling the 2019-2020 season. "As soon as it’s safe, we would like to finish our season. I’m ready and our team is ready. Nobody should be canceling anything," the four-time NBA MVP said a tweet sent on April 30.

Over on his Instagram feed, meanwhile, James is also making it clear to fans that he's still working hard in self-isolation to keep himself in peak physical condition should the season resume anytime soon. In his latest post, the three-time NBA champion broke down yet another of his grueling at-home workout routines, all while going shirtless and showing off his ripped six-pack abs.

The video montage includes hanging knee raises, followed by a treadmill run, a sled push, dumbbell deadlifts, Romanian deadlifts, and slider hamstring curls. "Staying POISED, PRIMED, and PREPPED for whenever it’s time to punch again," James wrote in a caption to the post.

James hasn't been shy lately about sharing insight into his at-home workouts—whether it's a cable-centric core-crushing routine, or thrashing the hell out of some battle ropes.
