Common Causes of Low Testosterone in Military Members

Several factors can contribute to low testosterone levels in military members, including:

Age-related decline: As men age, their testosterone production naturally decreases, a process known as andropause. This gradual decline can start as early as the late 20s or early 30s, with more significant drops occurring in later years.

Chronic stress and sleep deprivation: The demanding nature of military service often involves high levels of stress and disrupted sleep patterns, particularly during deployments or intense training periods. Chronic stress and lack of quality sleep can negatively impact testosterone production.
