Writing Books

You can write a book about any wellness topic, including your journey as a health and wellness business leader. Several books offer advice on writing and publishing a wellness book, so read those and start writing some of your thoughts in a notebook. Once you have a few pages of thoughts, read them and see if any themes or recurring elements stand out. Focus on those recurring ideas and flesh them out in more detail.

You can self-publish your book by printing out copies of it or posting it online. However, 90% of self-published books sell less than 100 copies, and 20% of self-published authors make no income from their work at all. If you want thousands of people to read your book, you must find an agent and publisher first. You can connect with agents by visiting literary conferences and sending query letters to agents in the wellness industry.

Very few wellness writers make all of their income from books. You should think of writing as a way to supplement your income and build your personal reputation as a wellness expert.
