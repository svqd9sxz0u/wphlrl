# Kit Harington Says He's Ready to Be a Dad Now That Game of Thrones Is Behind Him

[Thrones Is Behind Him](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/blog/6739445664.html)

As the heroic Jon Snow in HBO’s epic fantasy hit Game of Thrones, Kit Harington has faced down White Walkers, fallen in love, and even come back from the dead. But with the show entering its final season this Sunday, the actor already has his eyes on his next adventure— fatherhood.

[to Be Dad](https://matrix-skin.xianfengchem.com/)

“The most important job I’ll ever have is about to finish,” he said in a recent interview with InStyle, adding: “Well, not the most. Hopefully, I’ll be a father.”

Harington is married to actress Rose Leslie, who he met when she was cast as his love interest, the wildling Ygritte, back in Season 2. “If you’re already attracted to someone, and then they play your love interest in the show, it becomes very easy to fall in love,” Harington told Vogue Italia back in 2016.

Jon and Ygritte didn’t exactly get a happy ending together in the world of the show (after an incredibly tumultuous relationship, Ygritte died in Jon’s arms), but their real life romance appears to be going far more smoothly. However, Harington isn’t sure whether they’ll ever appear on-screen together after Game of Thrones.

“I think almost the worst thing about falling in love with Rose and marrying her is that it’s going to be very hard to work with her again,” he said. “Working opposite her was one of the highlights of my life and career. I don’t know when, if ever, I’m going to get to do that again, because we’re married now, and it’s hard to work opposite your wife.”

Acting with Leslie might be off the table now, but considering that offhand remark about becoming a father, it sounds like Harington has another project in mind for them as a couple.
