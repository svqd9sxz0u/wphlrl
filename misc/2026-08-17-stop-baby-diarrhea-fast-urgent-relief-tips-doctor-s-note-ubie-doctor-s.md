Internal Medicine

Diarrhea in babies can be managed by prioritizing rehydration to prevent fluid loss. Offer an oral rehydration solution, such as a diluted juice-water mixture, in small, frequent sips. In severe cases, intravenous fluids administered by a healthcare provider may be necessary. Continue breastfeeding or formula feeding as tolerated, and monitor for signs of dehydration like dry mouth, fewer wet diapers, or lethargy.

Because infant diarrhea can escalate quickly and stem from many different causes—ranging from mild viral infections to more serious conditions—understanding the underlying issue is critical to choosing the right care path.
