Internal Medicine

A dull sensation in the upper right abdomen is often linked to gallbladder or liver conditions. Common causes include gallstones, cholecystitis (gallbladder inflammation), fatty liver disease, hepatitis, or bile duct issues. Less commonly, it may signal problems with the right kidney, pancreas, or intestines.

Explanation

When you feel a dull ache in the upper right belly, the cause might be related to the gallbladder or liver. Here are some common reasons:

Gallstones: Tiny, hard pieces that form in the gallbladder can block ducts and cause discomfort or pain. This is sometimes known as biliary colic.

Cholecystitis: When the gallbladder becomes inflamed, usually because of a gallstone blocking it, you may feel a steady, dull ache.

Liver Problems: Conditions such as hepatitis (inflammation of the liver) or fatty liver disease can also make your right upper quadrant feel tender or uncomfortable.

Other Possibilities: Sometimes, muscle strain or other nearby organ issues may feel like they are coming from this area as well.
