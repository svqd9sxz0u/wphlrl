Ankle mobility can be improved through targeted exercises, stretching routines, and assistive devices such as pneumatic muscle and spring systems. Lengthening the Achilles tendon—either through consistent stretching or, in some cases, surgical intervention—can also significantly increase ankle range of motion and function.

Because limited ankle mobility can stem from many underlying causes (tight tendons, joint stiffness, past injuries, or neurological factors), identifying the root issue is essential before choosing a treatment path.
