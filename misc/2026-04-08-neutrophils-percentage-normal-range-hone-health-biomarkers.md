# Neutrophils Percentage Normal Range - Hone Health Biomarkers

What is Neutrophil Percentage?

Neutrophil percentage reflects the balance of neutrophils relative to other white blood cells. Neutrophils are a type of white blood cell produced in the bone marrow and released into the bloodstream. When the body detects infection, inflammation, or tissue damage, neutrophils are among the first immune cells to respond.

A normal neutrophil percentage generally indicates a balanced immune response, while shifts in this value can signal that the immune system is responding to infection, inflammation, or physiological stress.

Low Neutrophil (%)

Low neutrophil percentage can occur when neutrophils decrease or when other white blood cells increase. It is commonly associated with viral infections, autoimmune conditions, bone marrow disorders, certain medications, or nutrient deficiencies. Symptoms are caused by the underlying infection, not the marker.
