Bodybuilder Jon Call (a.k.a. Jujimufu) and his YouTube channel partner Tom Boyden of Juji & Tom will try anything for a fitness challenge: they've attempted unconventional deadlift variations, calisthenic power moves, ring muscleups, and even jiu jitsu with World's Strongest Man Martins Licis. Now, they're getting down to bodybuilding basics and going old school, trying out Arnold Schwarzenegger's chest workout. Chest is Call's "genetic weak point", so might as well try the best of the best.

Arnold has outlined a number of chest routines over the years, especially from his bodybuilding heyday. According to Schwarzenegger's Encyclopedia of Modern Bodybuilding, his early chest workout looked something like this:

Bench Press - 5 sets of 6 to 10 repsIncline Bench Presses - 5 sets of 6 to 10 repsFlat Bench Flys - 5 sets of 6 to 10 repsDips - 5 sets of 6 to 10 repsDumbbell Pullovers - 5 sets of 6 to 10 reps

Eventually, however, Schwarzenegger switched up the routine to a "more intense methodology of supersetting back with chest" for more volume, according to Boyden.

The workout Call follows is composed of four supersets followed by a "ribcage expander" of dumbbell pullovers, something that Schwarzenegger "really believed in."

Superset 1Bench Press - 5 sets of 6 repsBehind the Neck Pulldowns* - 5 sets of 15 reps

*If you want to try this workout yourself, perform standard lat pulldowns instead to be safer for your shoulders.

Superset 2Incline Press - 5 sets of 10 to 15 repsT-bar Rows - 5 sets of 10 to 15 reps

Superset 3Chest Flys - 5 sets of 10 to 15 repsWide Grip Bent Over Barbell Rows - 5 sets of 10 to 15 reps

Superset 4Dips - 5 sets of 15 repsChinups - 5 of 12 reps

Dumbbell Pullovers - 5 sets of 20 reps

He also notes that Arnold would workout twice a day, following a push-pull schedule.

"All those guys had a really high volume of training," says Call.

As they do the workout, they start to see results immediately.
