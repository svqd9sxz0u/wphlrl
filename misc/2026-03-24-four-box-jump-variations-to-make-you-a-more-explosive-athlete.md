# ​Four Box Jump Variations to Make You a More Explosive Athlete

Plyometric training is arguably the most effective way to become a more explosive athlete. And plyometric exercises like box jumps can even help you break through a plateau in one or more of your other lifts, like the squat.

In the video above, Bandana Training founder and Rumble Boxing founding trainer Rob Sulaver stops by the MH Rec Room to demonstrate four different variations of the box jump.

Rather than relying solely on the traditional box jump, and raising the box little by little as your power and explosivity improve, you can use these dynamic progressions to add variety to your training, resulting in faster gains—and more interesting workouts.

You’ll also remove the need to continuously seek out taller and taller boxes onto which to jump—great for days when equipment is scarce.

“I see a lot of guys in the gym doing traditional plyometric training, which is awesome—I love plyometric training,” Sulaver says. “But they usually progress it by hopping onto a higher and higher surface. That’s okay, but I want to give you some different variables you can play with.”

Watch Sulaver perform four different variations of the box jump, then apply them to your own training.

“I would suggest using each of these for a week in your plyometric training,” Sulaver says. “Progress from the non-counter movement, to the counter movement, to the double-contact movement, to the continuous movement.”
