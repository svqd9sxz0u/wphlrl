# 6 Guys Who Got Their Style All Wrong at Last Night's VMAs

While you were tuning into last night's Game of Thrones, the annual MTV Video Music Awards happened. Katy Perry soared in dressed as a moonman. Kendrick Lamar performed and won Video of the Year for "Humble." Taylor Swift debuted a new video. When it comes to the VMAs, there's no shortage of unforgettable, iconic moments, from the performances to the speeches. And that also includes the good—and especially the bad—fashion choices. Here, six looks we'd rather forget.

Ed Sheeran

[Their Style](https://dermaloom.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/5896770847.html)

The VMAs aren't a terribly formal event, but that's the beauty: You get to show off your personal style without suiting up. Problem is the only thing 26-year-old Artist of the Year winner Ed Sheeran showed us was how little he gives a crap about the VMAs—or his personal appearance. That T-shirt-and-glasses combo isn't even appropriate to go pick up pizza for a night in. But damn, we still have "Shape of You" stuck in our heads.

Logic

[Got Their](https://benpepsi.com/)

Rapper Logic got us all with his emotional speech about mental health and equality, but everyone was still scratching their heads over his look, which was part Silicon Valley bro, part 90s tourist. What's in that fanny pack, man?

[Their Style](https://dermaloom.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Joe Jonas

Showing off a new look with his band, DNCE, Joe Jonas had the right idea with a well-tailored dinner jacket—but totally lost us at his bedazzled shirt and lapels. Had he been a glitzy, glam-rock kinda guy beforehand instead of a too-cool kid you'd spot at fashion week, we might let it slide. To be honest, the mustache put it over the edge. Someone tell him this isn't the set of Magnum PI.

Jeremy Scott

If you're a 42-year-old dude wearing a gold sequined motorcycle jacket, you better have the style chops to pull it off. Instead, in yet another epic fail that makes our eyes bleed, designer Jeremy Scott looks flashy and out of touch—like a suburban dad who dressed up to retrieve his daughter from a concert so the kids would think he's "hip."
